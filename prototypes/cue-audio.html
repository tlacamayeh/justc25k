Cue Audio Test

Does this phone play the session's Cues reliably while Spotify is playing? This page checks three things:

  1. Ducking: do the short cue sounds lower your music and let it come back, instead of pausing it?
  2. Screen off: do Cues still play after you press the power button?
  3. App switching: do Cues still play while you're in Spotify?

Each phase change plays an Announcement (a 1.8 s placeholder sound standing in for the voice), then a Countdown of three soft chimes, one per second, then a Change Beep. The page can play them in three ways; try each one.
