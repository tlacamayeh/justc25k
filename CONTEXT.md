# justc25k

A personal Couch to 5K trainer that walks one runner through the plan, session by session, with cues they can follow mid-run without looking at the phone.

## Language

### The plan

**Plan**:
The fixed, canonical Couch to 5K program: 9 weeks of run/walk training ending in a continuous 5K run.
_Avoid_: Program, schedule, course

**Week**:
One of the Plan's nine numbered stages, each made of several Sessions.

**Session**:
One outing in the Plan, identified by its Week and its position within that Week (e.g. Week 3, Session 2).
_Avoid_: Workout, run, day

**Phase**:
One timed, uninterrupted stretch of a Session, of kind Warm-up, Run, Walk or Cool-down. A Session is an ordered list of Phases.
_Avoid_: Interval, segment, step

### Cues

**Cue**:
A sound that tells the runner what is happening without them looking at the phone. Every Phase change is signalled by an Announcement, then a Countdown, then a Change Beep.

**Announcement**:
The spoken Cue, about 5 seconds before a Phase change, naming the next Phase's kind and duration (e.g. "Walk, 90 seconds"), or "Session complete" before the end.
_Avoid_: Callout, prompt

**Countdown**:
Three soft chimes, one per second, in the last 3 seconds of a Phase.

**Change Beep**:
The single beep marking the instant one Phase ends and the next begins, or the Session ends.

### Progress

**Next Session**:
The Session the runner is due to do next. It advances when a Session is completed, and the runner can move it back to repeat a Session or forward to skip one.

**Completed Session**:
A Session whose Cool-down ran to the end, or which the runner ended early and chose to mark as done. Completing a Session advances the Next Session.

**Abandoned Session**:
A Session that was left without being completed. The Next Session does not change.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, failed
